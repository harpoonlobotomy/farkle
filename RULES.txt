
FARKLE RULES:

At the start of each turn, you roll the dice.
You can 'hold' any combination of dice from the scoring list below.
If you cannot take any dice, you bust.
After holding your chosen dice, you can either end your turn, or
reroll the remaining dice to try to get more points.
If you use all 6 dice, you can reroll all dice and keep building score.
Repeat rolling and holding until you end your turn or bust.

SCORING:

1 = 100 points
5 = 50 points
1, 1, 1 = 1000 points
1, 1, 1, 1 = 2000 points

THREE OF A KIND:
<number> x 100
[ eg  4, 4, 4 = 400 points ]

FOUR OF A KIND:
<number> x 100 x 2
[eg  4, 4, 4, 4 = 800 points ]

STRAIGHTS:
1, 2, 3, 4, 5 = 750 points
2, 3, 4, 5, 6 = 750 points
1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 = 1500 points
